Case Study 17: Problem Statement, Grading Rubrics, Algorithm & Python Implementation

Download student_performance_tracker.py

1. Problem Statement

In educational institutions, assessing students through periodic examinations without analyzing their longitudinal performance trajectories causes critical learning gaps to remain undetected until summative final exams. This project delivers an automated, pure-Python analytical pipeline that tracks score progression, pinpoints consistently failing subjects, and categorizes students for targeted pedagogical support.

2. Pedagogical Classification Rubric

Classification Category Quantitative Decision Criteria Target Academic Action
Consistent High Performer Overall Average ≥ 75.0% AND Net Trajectory ≥ 0.0% AND Zero Weak Subjects Nominate for Dean's Honor Roll and advanced research mentoring.
Progressive / Improving Net Progression ≥ +5.0% AND Final Exam Average ≥ 50.0% Encourage upward momentum and positive study habits.
Consistent Average Performer Stable scores between 50.0% and 75.0% with minimal fluctuations Targeted subject tutoring to propel student toward distinction.
Declining / Needs Attention Net Progression ≤ -5.0% between first and final examinations Urgent academic counselling to identify distress factors.
Critical / Remedial Required Final Exam Average < 50.0% OR ≥ 2 Consistently Weak Subjects Mandatory diagnostic remedial tutoring and parental notice.
